The Foundation Program
12-week path

SOLIDWORKS & CAD

Twelve weeks in SOLIDWORKS. Students learn the basics, model one object, and try to produce a prototype. If a print is not ready, they still leave with a visualization they can use later. AI & Coding is a separate path.

Week 1

Measure, Then Open SOLIDWORKS

Measure a real object in millimeters, then open CAD Premium, set units, and save a part.

Week 2

Sketch Until It Locks

Add relations and dimensions until the sketch is fully defined, then extrude it.

Week 3

Cut, Hole, and Fillet

Sketch on a face, cut, place a Hole Wizard hole, fillet an edge, and change one number.

Week 4

Pattern, Mirror, and Revolve

Repeat features with a pattern or mirror, and build one round part with a revolve.

Week 5

Pick the Prototype

Choose one small object, dimension it, and mark the face that will sit on the printer bed.

Week 6

Model the Base

Sketch their object and extrude it to the real size.

Week 7

Make It the Object

Add the cuts and details until the model looks like the thing they chose.

Week 8

Change a Number

Edit one dimension, rebuild, and fix the model so the file is usable later.

Week 9

Ready to Print

Thicken thin walls, set the bed face, and export an STL.

Week 10

Try a Prototype

Print it if a machine is free. Otherwise slice the STL and confirm it will print.

Week 11

A Picture for Later

Make one clear visualization of the object they can show when a print is not in hand.

Week 12

Capstone — Product Demo Day

Show the model, the picture, and the prototype or the STL.

AI & Coding is a separate 12-week path. See that sequence.
