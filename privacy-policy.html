American Foster Futures · AFF Futures mobile app

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026 · Last updated: July 21, 2026

American Foster Futures (“AFF,” “we,” “us”) provides the AFF Futures mobile application to help foster youth and supporters access education and transition tools and participate in tuition-waiver advocacy. This policy describes what the app collects and how we use it.

1. Overview

  • You can use most of the app without creating an account.
  • Progress (state, checklist, college steps) is stored on your device by default.
  • Optional features (cloud help/volunteer submissions, account, encrypted vault backup) send data to our servers only when you use them.

2. Information we collect

On your device only

  • Onboarding choices (role, state, primary need)
  • Checklist and college-path progress
  • Campaign action counts
  • FAFSA reminder preference and locally scheduled notifications
  • Encrypted vault contents and vault passphrase (passphrase is never sent to AFF)

If you submit help or volunteer forms

  • Name, contact info, state, message/roles you provide
  • We use this to respond and operate programs

If you create an optional account

  • Email address
  • Password (stored as a secure hash; we cannot see your password)

If you enable encrypted vault cloud backup

  • Encrypted document ciphertext, IV, and category labels
  • We cannot read vault titles, notes, or file contents — encryption keys are derived from your vault passphrase on your device

Technical data

  • Standard server logs (IP address, timestamps) when you contact our API
  • We do not sell personal information

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, FAFSA and college reminders are scheduled locally on your device using dates from our FAFSA calendar. You can turn them off in the app or system settings.

4. How we use information

  • Provide and improve the app and AFF programs
  • Respond to help and volunteer requests
  • Maintain optional account and vault backup features
  • Protect against abuse and secure our systems

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who host our API (e.g. cloud hosting) under contracts that limit use to providing the service, or when required by law. Public campaign stories or media contact are opt-in only.

6. Children & youth

The app is designed primarily for foster youth roughly ages 16–23 and supportive adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided data, contact us to delete it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures including HTTPS and password hashing. Vault documents are encrypted on-device before any cloud backup. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; protect your device and vault passphrase.

8. Your choices

  • Reset local app data from the Me tab
  • Delete vault items or lock the vault
  • Sign out or request account deletion by emailing us
  • Disable notifications in system settings
