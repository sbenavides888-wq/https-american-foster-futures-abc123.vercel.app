American Foster Futures · Vehicle Access

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026 · Last updated: September 21, 2026

American Foster Futures (“AFF,” “we,” “us”) provides the Vehicle Access app for a demonstration of the Foster Youth Priority Vehicle Access Program, a proposed Texas surplus-vehicle priority for eligible foster youth ages 18 through 22. This beta is not a live vehicle sale. AFF is not the statutory program administrator and does not take title to vehicles or payment for them.

1. Summary

  • An account is required to open the demo. The account is created on this device only.
  • We do not send your name, email, password, city, profile photo, or documents you attach to AFF servers in this beta.
  • We do not sell personal information, and the app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • You can delete the account and the personal information stored by the app from inside the app.

2. Information stored on your device

  • First and last name, email address, and Texas city
  • A salted hash of your password. The password itself is not stored.
  • The role you choose (partner briefing, youth buyer, or program desk)
  • Eligibility answers, including a yes or no that you are 18 through 22. The app does not ask for your date of birth.
  • A profile photo, if you choose one
  • Files you choose to attach, such as a license, insurance, or funds document
  • Vehicle-interest notes for the demo catalog

Those items stay in the app’s storage on the phone. The program-desk role on the same device can open files you attached. We cannot see them. Deleting the app removes them. Do not attach a real driver’s license or other identity document unless you are comfortable with it remaining on that phone until you delete the account or the app.

3. Information this beta does not collect

  • Date of birth, Social Security number, or phone number
  • Precise location, contacts, health information, or payment card numbers
  • Information from children under 13

The program described in the app is for ages 18 through 22 and for adults reviewing the briefing. We do not knowingly allow a child under 13 to create an account. If you believe that happened, email us and delete the app from the device.

4. Photos

The app asks for photo library access only when you tap to set a profile picture or attach a document photo. It does not read your photo library in the background, and it does not use the camera.

5. Notifications and updates

This beta does not schedule notifications and does not ask for notification permission. The installed app may contact Expo’s update service with the app version, platform, and an anonymous update identifier so it can download a bug-fix update. That check is not tied to your name, email, or documents, and it is not used to track you across other apps.

6. Links you open

The app can open the Texas Facilities Commission surplus store, a public vehicle listing, or americanfosterfutures.org in the browser. A lot screen may show the surplus store’s published phone number. Those sites and that phone line are not operated by American Foster Futures. We do not receive the contents of calls or of pages you open.

7. Account deletion

In the app, open Me → Account settings → Delete account. That permanently removes the account from the device, including your name, email, profile photo, and documents the app stored. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Sign out only ends the session. It does not delete the account. You may also email info@americanfosterfutures.org with a privacy question. Because this beta does not send account data to us, we do not have a server copy to delete.

8. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share the on-device account with DFPS, a surplus agency, or other users. A later live version of the program would require an updated policy before any information left the device.

9. Security

The session token is stored in the device keychain when the operating system provides one. No method of storage on a phone is perfect. Protect the device passcode, and do not leave the app signed in on a shared phone.

10. Contact

Web: www.americanfosterfutures.org

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new date at the top of this page. If a later build sends personal information off the device, this policy and the App Store privacy labels will be updated before that build is distributed.
