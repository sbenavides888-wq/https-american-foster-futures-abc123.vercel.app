About us

The people.

Stuart and Ruth Carlson were both raised overseas as third-generation missionary kids — Stuart in Peru and Ruth in Nigeria. In 2000 they created Partners in Joy, Inc., in Duncanville, Texas.

Stuart Carlson

Director and President

Stuart was raised in Peru as a third-generation missionary kid. In the late 1990s he and Ruth began partnering with Joi Ministries in Nigeria, later renamed Alternorm Ministries. In 2000 they formed Partners in Joy, Inc., in Duncanville, Texas, so they could communicate with partner ministries and receive and send support.

Ruth Carlson

Historian. Liaison for Alternorm and Ministry of Mercy.

Ruth was raised in Nigeria as a third-generation missionary kid. She and Stuart founded Partners in Joy in 2000. She serves as the organization’s historian and as liaison for Alternorm Ministries and Ministry of Mercy.

Esther Nordman

Secretary and Treasurer. Liaison for the Igala Language Project.

Esther keeps the books of Partners in Joy and is the liaison for the Igala Language Project. Booklet orders are sent to the Partners in Joy address with the booklet title on the memo line. A 2014 field note thanks Esther and Ruth for planning a visit among the Igala people.

Judy Reed

Board member. Liaison for the Reed ministry and the Porras Ministry.

Judy grew up in Peru and served as a linguist and Bible translator. She and Robert met in college, served 14 years in Colombia, where their two daughters were born, and then more than 30 years in Dallas. Robert was a founding faculty member at Dallas International University and taught for 26 years at Dallas Baptist University. They joined Partners in Joy in 2016 and served with ProMETA in Costa Rica. Robert finished his earthly race in April 2026. Judy continues that encouragement, the preparation of his courses, and her relationships in Churingaveni, Peru.

Sonnie Ebikwo

Board member

Sonnie Ebikwo serves on the board of Partners in Joy. The current public page lists that role.

Pauline Rappold

Board member. Liaison for the Jesus Film.

Pauline Rappold serves on the board and is the liaison for the Jesus Film.

Joseph Ogijoh

Board member. Liaison for the Moses and Alexis Abraham ministry.

Joseph Ogijoh serves on the board and is the liaison for Planting Brighter Futures, the ministry of Moses and Alexis Abraham. That ministry mentors Nigerian young men ages 14 to 24, with a vision centered on Kogi State.

Cristy Millington is a liaison for Dr. Isaac Ogohi and CMML Trinity Bible College in central Nigeria.

In memoriam · June 22, 1954 – April 18, 2026

Dr. Robert Reed

Robert grew up in Colombia as a second-generation missionary. He and Judy joined Partners in Joy in 2016. He was a founding faculty member at Dallas International University, taught for 26 years at Dallas Baptist University, and served with ProMETA.

Ruth Carlson wrote, on the current contact page: “Thank you, Dr. Robert Reed, for serving so faithfully on the board of Partners in Joy. Your insight and advice were invaluable. Well done, good and faithful servant.” Matthew 25:21.
