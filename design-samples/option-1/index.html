Partners in Joy

“Just as there are many parts to our bodies, so it is with Christ’s body… for we each have a different work to do… each needs all the others.”

Romans 12:4–5

We are a group of Bible-believing Christians with a common passion who have become partners for a greater vision. We come alongside ministries that are already at work.

Vision

To share the grace of the Lord Jesus Christ, the love of the Father, and the unity of the Holy Spirit with as many people as possible. (2 Corinthians 13:14)

Mission

We assist established ministries as they expand the Kingdom of God and fulfill the Great Commission.

How we partner

  • Receive and send funding and materials
  • Offer ideas and expertise as we can
  • Pray
  • Connect people

Partner ministries

Alternorm Ministries

Arts and media in Jos, Nigeria. Begun in 1997 as Joi Ministries. The fence and gate on this land are in place, toward offices, studios, storage, and an event center.

Planting Brighter Futures

Moses and Alexis Abraham mentor Nigerian young men ages 14–24. The vision is centered on Kogi State.

Judy Reed Ministry

Global Ripples of Grace. Encouragement and training, including with ProMETA.

Ministry of Mercy

Orphanage homes, a school, and a medical center in Otutulu and Lokoja. Monthly updates on the current site describe more than 400 residents.

Trinity Bible College

CMML college in central Nigeria training pastors. More than 250 graduates.

Igala Language Project

Booklets that help Igala readers become fluent in the Bible they already have. $5 each, plus $1.25 shipping.

Decapolis

Regenerative agriculture. The page says more than 1,200 farmers have been trained since November 2023, and $35 sends one person to a training.

Porras Ministry

Manuel and Yorleny Porras train indigenous pastors and leaders in Costa Rica through CEI.

Short term trips

Groups have served in Nigeria for a number of years, often alongside Ministry of Mercy.
