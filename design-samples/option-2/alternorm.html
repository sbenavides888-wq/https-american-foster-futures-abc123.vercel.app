Creatively communicating the Gospel.

Alternorm is committed to creatively communicating the Gospel using the arts and media, and altering norms through Christ.

Music, film, books, television, radio, and the internet shape what people treat as normal. Alternorm’s answer is to use those same tools for the glory of God. Activities named on the site include WINGS, SKOOP, Neighborhood Kiddies Hangout, Tehilla Hour, the Ibaji Liberation Crusade Series, and Christovibes.

On the Alternorm site

WINGS

The WINGS concert series uses music, dance, drama, stories, and illustration. The point is the Gospel of Christ, not entertainment alone.

On the Alternorm site

SKOOP

Schools Outreach Project. Teams visit schools at no cost to the school, with music, theatre, dance, humor, and a short message.

On the Alternorm site

Neighborhood Kiddies Hangout

The same teams also go into neighborhoods. The site says this is because Jesus loves the children there.

On the Alternorm site

Ibaji Liberation Crusades

A collaboration with Ibaji Liberation Outreach Mission in the Ibaji community of Kogi State, coordinated with CAN, PFN, and ministers of the area. The public site lists a March 2026 edition.

On the Alternorm site

Tehilla Hour

Praise and worship, bimonthly or quarterly, on the last Sunday of the month, with the Beautiful Gate Zone of the Redeemed Christian Church of God, Plateau Province 4, Jos. Soundalive, Graciah, and Joe Ifah are regulars, with the Beautiful Gate Voices and other RCCG choirs.

A place in Jos

The fence and gate are done. The hope described on the Partners in Joy page is offices, studios, storage, and an event center.

Christovibes and support

Christovibes is an online radio station on the Alternorm site, Christian music from Africa and the rest of the world, 24 hours, at christovibes.com.

A gift in US dollars on the live Alternorm site is received by Partners in Joy. A gift in naira stays on Alternorm. A check should say Alternorm. Ruth Carlson is the liaison. Joi7 Premises, Hwolshe, Jos, Nigeria 930253. This preview does not accept the gift.
