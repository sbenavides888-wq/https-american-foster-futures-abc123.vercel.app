The Foundation Program

Inspired by Asimov’s Foundation — building leaders who code, grow, and reach for the stars. Our north star: a foster youth in space.

Apply or volunteer Explore the pillars

The Foundation Program is AFF’s pipeline for young people who have experienced foster care or other disadvantage. We pair hands-on AI coding, modular microgreens (life-support skills for the future), and college-to-career pathways.

Three frontiers

Code. Grow. Launch.

AI & coding

Hands-on AI training

Practical experience with modern AI tools and agents — build, problem-solve, and create. The same energy as AI-assisted development environments used by builders and engineers worldwide.

Foundation Farms

Modular microgreens

Hands-on indoor grow work with modular microgreen systems — compact food production skills relevant to life-support and farming concepts for space travel, developed through Foundation Farms (Dallas, TX).

Space exploration

Reach for the stars

STEM career exposure and mentorship aimed at technology and space exploration — with an ambitious long-term goal of sending a foster youth or former foster youth to space.

Why “Foundation”?

Build for the long term

Named for Isaac Asimov’s Foundation series — preserving knowledge, building for generations, and shaping the future through science and leadership. We invest in young people today so they can guide tomorrow.

  • Inspired by Asimov — institutions and skills that outlast any single generation.
  • A foster youth in space — a defining goal that motivates rigorous STEM preparation.
  • Advancing technology — AI and emerging fields so participants help shape what comes next.
  • Life-support skills — modular microgreens for sustainability and space farming concepts.
  • Leadership for the overlooked — equipping youth who have faced foster care to lead.
What you receive

Same core benefits as the AFF Action app

Hands-on AI training

Practical experience with modern AI tools and agents to learn how to build, problem-solve, and create with AI.

Modular microgreens

Indoor grow work with modular systems — compact food production skills for life-support and space farming concepts.

College navigation

Support applying to strong universities, securing financial aid, and choosing STEM pathways. See resources.

Post-graduation placement

Résumé coaching, internships, and job search support for roles in AI, tech, and space exploration.

Participants also gain STEM career exposure and mentorship from professionals in technology, research, and the space industry.

Who it’s for

Ages 14–24 · STEM curious · No experience required

Current and former foster youth and other disadvantaged young people interested in STEM — especially AI and space exploration. Curiosity and commitment matter most.
