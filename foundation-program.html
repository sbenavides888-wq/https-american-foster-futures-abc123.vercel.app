The Foundation Program

Inspired by Asimov’s Foundation — building leaders who code, design, grow, and reach for the stars. Our north star: a foster youth in space.

Apply or volunteer 3D CAD with SOLIDWORKS Explore the pillars

The Foundation Program is AFF’s STEM pipeline for young people who have experienced foster care or other disadvantage. We pair hands-on AI coding, 3D CAD design with SOLIDWORKS, modular microgreens (life-support skills for the future), and college-to-career pathways.

Four frontiers

Code. Design. Grow. Launch.

AI & coding

Hands-on AI training

Practical experience with modern AI tools and agents — build, problem-solve, and create. Learn to build a website, a multi-function phone application, and other software. The same energy as AI-assisted development environments used by builders and engineers worldwide.

3D CAD

SOLIDWORKS design

Professional 3D CAD training using SOLIDWORKS AI, Design, Simulation, Electronics, and Plastics — model, test, and prototype real products. See the CAD track →

Foundation Farms

Modular microgreens

Hands-on indoor grow work with modular microgreen systems — compact food production skills relevant to life-support and farming concepts for space travel, developed through Foundation Farms (Dallas, TX).

Space exploration

Reach for the stars

STEM career exposure and mentorship aimed at technology and space exploration — with an ambitious long-term goal of sending a foster youth or former foster youth to space.

New track

3D CAD design using SOLIDWORKS

Through a SOLIDWORKS for Good grant, Foundation Program participants have access to professional 3D CAD software used in aerospace, manufacturing, and product design. Youth learn to take an idea from sketch to a tested digital prototype — the same tools working engineers use every day.

Grant access includes SOLIDWORKS AI, Design, Simulation, Electronics, and Plastics. No prior CAD experience is required.

AI

AI-assisted modeling and design workflows that speed up parts, drawings, and iteration.

Design

3D parts, assemblies, and drawings — the core of professional mechanical CAD.

Simulation

Test strength, motion, and performance virtually before anything is built.

Electronics

Circuit and electrical design alongside the mechanical model.

Plastics

Plastic-part and injection-molding analysis for real-world manufacturing.

Foundation Builders

What each week covers

A 12-week lab, one session at a time. Weeks 1–6: AI, a live website, and a publishable phone app. Weeks 7–12: geometry, 3D CAD with SOLIDWORKS, a print-ready part, and Product Demo Day.

Weeks 1–6 · AI, websites, and software

Mission 1
Week 1

What are Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence

Explain AI vs. machine learning, and compute a simple neuron by hand.

Week 2

Introduction to Agentic AI and Project Development

Learn agentic AI, choose a product, and map the first project.

Week 3

AI Prompts and Loops to Build Websites, Apps and Software

Use prompt loops to build a working website on their machine.

Week 4

Push a Live Website to the Internet

Publish the page to a public URL.

Week 5

Phone App and Software Development

Build original software that runs on a real phone.

Week 6

Finalize and Push a Live Phone App or Software

Finish a publishable app: installable build and store packet.

Weeks 7–12 · 3D CAD and Product Demo Day

Mission 2
Week 7

Geometry for Working in 2D and 3D Environments

Measure, sketch, and locate objects in 2D and 3D — no CAD software yet.

Week 8

The World Has a Z

Build a 3D model you can spin in Tinkercad.

Week 9

Design Like It Has to Survive

Choose a part and learn print vs. mold rules.

Week 10

How the Pros Model

Parametric CAD with SOLIDWORKS: sketch, dimension, constrain.

Week 11

Build the Part

Extrude, cut, and fillet a real part, then change a number.

Week 12

Capstone — Product Demo Day

Present the product: website, app, 3D part, or a bundle.

Why “Foundation”?

Build for the long term

Named for Isaac Asimov’s Foundation series — preserving knowledge, building for generations, and shaping the future through science and leadership. We invest in young people today so they can guide tomorrow.

  • Inspired by Asimov — institutions and skills that outlast any single generation.
  • A foster youth in space — a defining goal that motivates rigorous STEM preparation.
  • Advancing technology — AI and emerging fields so participants help shape what comes next.
  • 3D CAD with SOLIDWORKS — professional Design, AI, Simulation, Electronics, and Plastics tools for building real products.
  • Life-support skills — modular microgreens for sustainability and space farming concepts.
  • Leadership for the overlooked — equipping youth who have faced foster care to lead.
Who it’s for

Ages 14–24 · STEM curious · No experience required

Current and former foster youth and other disadvantaged young people interested in STEM — especially AI, 3D CAD, and space exploration. Curiosity and commitment matter most.
