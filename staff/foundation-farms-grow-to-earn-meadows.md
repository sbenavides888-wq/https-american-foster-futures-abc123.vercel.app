## One sentence

Foundation Farms provides paid vocational training for current and former foster youth in Dallas: they learn to farm modular microgreens, are hired as paid associates, sell the crop to DFW restaurants, and 100% of proceeds return to youth wages and the next cohort.

## Why Meadows — Education and Human Services

Grow to Earn is **paid vocational training** in **two Meadows program areas**:

| Area | How this program fits | Meadows cousins |
|---|---|---|
| **Education** | Paid vocational training: 12-week curriculum, Texas Food Handler credential, documented hours, pathway into DFW food jobs or further training | Per Scholas ($50k, North Texas technical education); Out Teach ($100k, North Texas hands-on science) |
| **Human Services** | Direct support for current/former foster youth (16–24): paid vocational training, first employer, wages, transport help, 90-day retention | Café Momentum ($250k, high-barrier youth + real production); VolunteerNow ($200k, Texas youth 16–24 not in school/work); Wilkinson Center ($50k, Dallas livable-wage jobs) |

$100,000 is a first-time ask in the Out Teach / Per Scholas band, not a capital campaign. In narrative, keep repeating **Education and Human Services** so the assigned officer can place it in either (or both).

## What to say / not say

**Lead with:** paid vocational training, Education and Human Services, Dallas, ages 16–24, Texas Food Handler, restaurant buyers, 100% proceeds reinvested, 90-day retention.

**Do not lead with:** A foster youth in space, California HQ, tuition-waiver policy, scholarships, a new building.

**Texas test:** Every dollar of this grant is spent on Dallas training, Dallas wages, DFW sales, and Texas youth. HQ may be California; the *project* is Texas.

## Year-one model

| Item | Plan |
|---|---|
| Cohorts | 2 × 12 youth |
| Eligibility | Current/former foster care, 16–24, can reach Dallas shifts |
| Training | Paid vocational training: 12 weeks × 16 hours/week @ $20/hr |
| Credential | Texas Food Handler (100% of completers) |
| Hire | Completers offered Farm Associate @ $20/hr |
| Sales | DFW restaurants, caterers, other wholesale; 100% proceeds to AFF |
| Wraparound | Bus passes / shift timing (transport is the usual job-loss reason) |

### Outcomes (same as public page)

- 24 enrolled
- 80% complete training
- 100% of completers certified
- 70% hired as associates or placed with a DFW food employer
- 75% of those hired still working at 90 days
- 8 standing DFW buyer accounts
- 100% of sales proceeds returned to the program
